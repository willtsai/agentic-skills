# Go to Market

Translate validated product value into a credible adoption and launch system. Do not manufacture market evidence or promise unavailable capabilities.

## Inputs

- target users, buyers, influencers, and blockers
- customer and market evidence
- product value, scenarios, differentiation, and limitations
- launch scope, availability, regions, pricing, packaging, and timing constraints
- channel, sales, partner, support, documentation, and operational capabilities
- success measures and business goals

## Workflow

1. Define the launch objective and audience segments.
2. Distinguish user, buyer, administrator, partner, and influencer needs.
3. Articulate the category, problem, value, differentiation, proof, and limitations.
4. Map the journey from awareness through evaluation, activation, adoption, retention, and advocacy.
5. Select channels and motions based on evidence and audience behavior.
6. Identify required content, demos, documentation, training, sales, partner, support, and community enablement.
7. Assess product, operational, legal, localization, support, and measurement readiness.
8. Define staged rollout, experiments, feedback loops, guardrails, and stop conditions.
9. Assign owners and dependencies.
10. Identify claims that require validation before external use.

## Quality bar

- Positioning traces to evidence and shipped or committed capabilities.
- Each audience receives a relevant outcome, proof point, and call to action.
- Launch readiness includes support, documentation, operations, and measurement.
- Measures cover adoption and sustained value, not only impressions or launch activity.
- Risks include owners, mitigations, and stop conditions.

## Boundaries

- Own launch and adoption strategy, not unilateral product scope changes.
- Do not publish, contact customers, set pricing, or make external commitments without explicit authority.
- Use `customer-discovery` when audience or message evidence is weak.
- Use `developer-experience-review` for developer-facing products.
- Escalate material claims or commitments to `product-ceo` and, when appropriate, `board-review`.
